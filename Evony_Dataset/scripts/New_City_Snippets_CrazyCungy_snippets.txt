#-- 
#-- ATTENTION: These goals have only been tested on and can only be guaranteed
#-- to run on the latest versions of the RE bot so check the forum for updates.
#-- 
#-- Also, try not to rearrange or change the order of these goals because
#-- the goals are read and processed by the bot from top to bottom and changing
#-- the current order of some of the goals can break them and cause errors.

#-- For your convenience, the training hero goals are already configured and
#-- setup properly, you need only to change the values of the first three
#-- variables in the "Initial Setup" section at the top.
#-- There is also a variable named "warTown" there were you can enter the name
#-- of your wartown. This is for when you have a wartown that you will port often
#-- causing it to change coords and disrupt goal operations like troops/reso
#-- transfers and reinforcements. By entering the name of you wartown here the
#-- bot will get the current map location(coords) for the town name you enter and
#-- assign them to the variable %warTownloc% so you can use that variable in place
#-- of the coords for your wartown in goals. Example:
#--      keeptroop %warTownloc% a:500000 5000
#-- This becomes even more convienent as your wartown moves around the map more.
#-- These goals also utilize conditional values heavily. Some of these are based
#-- on which town it is, the town's name, number of troops, research levels,
#-- account age, etc. This will make it more simple because there is much less
#-- need to make changes to these goals since they will change for you. In order
#-- to ensure the changes take place, it is necessary to reload the goals every
#-- so often by simply pressing the "Set Goals" button again. You will need to do
#-- this once every hour for the first few hours of an account's life, then again
#-- about 3 hours later, then every 6 hours for the first 2-3 days, then about once
#-- a day for a couple weeks. After that a couple times a week maybe.
