there are also 2 ways to make the bot automaticly move resources ..

keepresource 101,101 g:10m
this goal will automaticly send all gold in excess over 10 mil to city at 101,101
You can set diff keepresource goals for each resource, or combine them like:
  keepresource 101,101 g:10m,f:10m,w:10m

requesthelp 101,101 s:1 s:1 g:5m g:25m
this gold will make the bot transport in the other directions ... 
if this city has less than 5 mil gold, and 101,101 has more than 25m gold
the bot will transport gold from 101,101 to this city
the s:1 s:1 are settings for troops
the requesthelp goal must have settings for both troop and resources
just using 1 scout to keep in tune with syntax of the goal
